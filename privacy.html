Privacy policy

Last updated September 15, 2026

Relvi ("we", "us") is a personal external-memory app: you capture things you don't want to forget — text, voice, photos, screenshots, documents, links — and Relvi uses AI to figure out what each one is and reminds you at the right time. This page explains exactly what we collect, why, and how to delete it.

Who we are

Relvi is developed by an independent developer. Contact us any time at support@relvi.app.

Information we collect

Relvi collects only what's needed to run the app for you:

  • Account information — your email address and password (handled by our authentication provider, Supabase; we never see your plaintext password).
  • Capture content — the text, voice recordings, photos/screenshots, documents, and links you save into Relvi, plus any title, category, date, person, or note that Relvi's AI extracts from them.
  • People you mention — names or aliases of people referenced in your captures (e.g. "lunch with Jane"), stored so Relvi can group related items. This is for your own organization only — it is never shared with or visible to anyone else.
  • Push notification token — a device identifier used to deliver reminders and your daily briefing.
  • App preferences — settings like notification toggles, reminder timing, and timezone.
  • Basic diagnostic and usage data — crash reports and coarse product-usage events (e.g. "a capture was created"), described further below. This never includes the content of your captures.

How your captures are processed by AI

When you save something, its content is sent to OpenAI's API to classify it (e.g. task, event, bill, reminder) and extract structured details like a date or amount. Voice captures are transcribed and photos/screenshots are read via OCR, both also through OpenAI's API, before that same classification happens. This processing happens on our server (not on OpenAI's own consumer products), and OpenAI does not use API content to train its models. A separate, optional AI feature ("Ask Relvi") lets you ask questions about your own captures in natural language; it only ever searches your own data and never another user's.

Where your data is stored

Your account and capture data are stored in a Supabase-hosted database and file storage, protected by row-level security so that only your own account can ever read or write your data — not even other Relvi users. Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS) and at rest.

Analytics and crash reporting

We use PostHog for basic product analytics and Sentry for crash/error reporting, both restricted to a fixed, reviewed list of events. These tools never receive the text of your captures, transcripts, OCR output, people's names, search queries, or any other capture content — only metadata like "a capture was created" or "the app crashed here." We don't use session replay or automatic screen recording. We don't sell your data or share it with advertisers, and we don't use your data to serve ads.

How long we keep your data

We keep your data for as long as your account exists. Deleting an individual capture or open loop removes it (and any attached file) immediately. You can permanently delete your entire account at any time from Settings → Privacy & Data → Delete account, which removes your account, every capture, attachment, and all associated data — this cannot be undone.

Your choices and rights

You can review and edit anything Relvi's AI extracted from a capture, and turn off individual notification types, right from within the app. There's no automated data export yet — email us at support@relvi.app to request a copy of your data or ask us to delete it on your behalf. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights to access, correct, or port your personal data under laws like the GDPR or CCPA; contact us and we'll honor applicable requests.

Children's privacy

Relvi is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us data, contact us and we'll delete it.

Changes to this policy

If we make material changes to how we handle your data, we'll update this page and revise the "Last updated" date above.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your data? Email support@relvi.app.
