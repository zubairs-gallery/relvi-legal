Delete your Relvi account

Account deletion happens directly inside the Relvi app — there's no separate web form. It's permanent and cannot be undone.

Steps to delete your account

  1. Open Relvi and sign in.
  2. Tap the account icon on the Today screen to open Settings.
  3. Go to Privacy & Data.
  4. Tap Delete account and confirm twice.

What gets deleted

  • Your account and login credentials
  • Every capture (text, voice, photos, documents, links) and any AI-extracted data from them
  • All attachments and uploaded files
  • People, tags, reminders, and search history associated with your account

Nothing is retained after deletion completes — this is a full, permanent removal, not a deactivation.

Can't access the app?

If you're unable to sign in to request deletion yourself, email support@relvi.app from the email address on your account and we'll delete it on your behalf.
